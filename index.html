How to use the kitchen

  • Get food: click an item in the pantry tray. It appears on the cutting board.
  • Hand (H): drag food to the pan, into the pot, onto the board or the plate.
  • Burners: set the flame for the pan and the pot in the panel on the right.
  • Tongs (T): click food to flip it.
  • Probe (P): click food to push a thermometer to its centre. The chart records it.
  • Knife (K): drag a line across food to cut it, and see the inside.
  • Time: speed up to 60×, or fast-forward until the probe reads a temperature.
  • Thermal view (V): colours every surface and cut face by temperature.

Heat really travels through each piece of food on a 1–3 mm grid. Browning, doneness, juice loss and food-safety flags come from sourced food science; see Sources.
